# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MALIS NAILST serves three connected user groups. Salon staff use the admin system during the workday to manage appointments, clients, services, payments, inventory, employees, and reports without missing urgent actions. The owner or manager uses the same system to understand daily performance, staffing, revenue, service demand, and operational risk. Clients use the public booking flow to understand services and request appointments with clear expectations before the salon confirms availability.

## Product Purpose

The product is a salon operations and online reservation system for MALIS NAILST. It exists to turn a visual prototype into a reliable working tool: the admin surface should answer what needs attention now, what happened today, and what action should happen next; the public surface should convert interested clients into clear booking requests. Success means fewer missed confirmations, cleaner scheduling, clearer cash and inventory decisions, and a booking experience that feels polished without hiding operational constraints.

## Brand Personality

Premium, calm, and precise. The interface should feel like a private back office for a focused beauty studio: polished enough to support the salon brand, restrained enough for daily repeated use, and clear enough that staff can act quickly under time pressure. The public voice should be warm and confident; the admin voice should be direct, useful, and action-oriented.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy beauty templates, neon/glam salon cliches, emoji-heavy UI, fake-perfect metrics, generic admin dashboards, over-decorated cards, decorative glass effects, and filler slogans. Do not make the admin feel like a marketing landing page. Do not hide operational truth behind luxury styling: pending appointments, stock risk, payment status, and missing data must stay visible.

## Design Principles

1. Put decisions before decoration: every dashboard and admin screen should lead with the next useful action.
2. Separate public trust from operational density: booking can be warmer and more visual, while admin stays compact and scannable.
3. Make status impossible to miss: confirmations, payments, stock levels, loading, errors, and empty states need explicit treatments.
4. Preserve calm premium restraint: use the salon identity to support confidence, not to add noise.
5. Design for real salon interruptions: forms, navigation, and actions should recover gracefully when staff are switching tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the baseline, with stronger contrast and interaction clarity where feasible. All core actions must be keyboard reachable, focus states must be visible, placeholder or helper text must maintain readable contrast, and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Spanish copy should be direct, readable, and resilient to longer labels or future localization. Color must never be the only way to communicate status.
